Experiment settings

Weights are calculated before the execution date. Trading occurs at the first eligible close; new weights earn returns afterward.
Method & assumptions

Adjusted-close returns approximate dividend reinvestment. The S&P 500 price index is the CAPM market proxy. Annual market return is mean daily return × 252.

Sharpe uses lagged 10-year Treasury yields as a daily cash-rate proxy. This is not a realized Treasury investment return.

Fixed stock selection may create survivorship bias. Short samples produce unstable annualized metrics. Historical data may be revised.

Costs, taxes, financing and slippage are zero. Turnover excludes the initial investment. Solver failures are shown explicitly.
