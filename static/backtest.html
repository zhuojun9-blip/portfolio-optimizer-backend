Method & assumptions

Adjusted-close returns approximate dividend reinvestment. Annualization uses 252 trading days. Costs, taxes, financing and slippage are zero. Turnover excludes the initial investment.

Fixed stock selection may create survivorship bias. Historical data may be revised. A maximum estimated Sharpe does not guarantee the highest realized Sharpe.
